Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

AI Business OS is a desktop-based application currently under development for creating, organizing, and managing short-form video content.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the enabled features, AI Business OS may process account identifiers, account authorization credentials, video files, captions, posting preferences, and publishing history.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to connect authorized accounts, prepare video content, verify posting destinations, and manage video uploads.

3. Third-Party Services

AI Business OS may connect with TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram. When authorized, information may be exchanged with these platforms to provide the requested features, subject to their own privacy policies.

4. Storage and Security

The application currently operates primarily on a local computer. Account authorization data and video-related records may be stored locally. Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect credentials against unauthorized access.

5. Retention and Deletion

Data may be retained while needed for the application's functions. Users may request information about deleting stored data or disconnecting their accounts through the contact information provided by the application operator.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as new features or integrations become available.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion, or account disconnection requests, contact the application operator using the contact information published on this website.
